### About

I work where strategy meets revenue. I'm trained in business strategy at Cardiff Business School and have worked in London and Lahore. My job is turning ambiguous commercial questions into numbers, plans and closed deals: pricing, market entry, pipeline design and the client relationships that keep revenue recurring.

I've founded and run my own food venture, built sales pipelines from zero, and modelled pricing for a UK manufacturer in a market where input costs moved daily. I care about clear thinking, clean spreadsheets and outcomes that can be measured.
